How a deck looks

It's just HTML.

One element per slide. Any class name works: section.slide, reveal.js, a web component, or plain <section>s.

Speaker notes go in an <aside class="notes">.

<section class="slide">
  <h2>It's just HTML.</h2>
  <p>One element per slide.</p>

  <!-- only you see this -->
  <aside class="notes">
    Mention the presenter view.
  </aside>
</section>

Two screens

Like PowerPoint's presenter view.

01

Audience

Full screen on the projector. Only the slide, nothing else.

02

You

Current and next slide, your notes, a timer and the clock.

03

Control

Arrows or a clicker. B for black, G for every slide, a number + Enter to jump.
